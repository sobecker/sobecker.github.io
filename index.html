Sophia Becker, PhD

Postdoc in Computational Neuroscience

I study what makes an experience worth learning from. Trained as a mathematician, I develop computational theories of novelty, curiosity and exploration, and test them against neural and behavioral data.

In my PhD with Wulfram Gerstner at EPFL, I showed that novelty is not a fixed property of a stimulus but depends on the learner's internal representation. I am now extending this work to how novelty shapes learning in humans and artificial neural networks.
