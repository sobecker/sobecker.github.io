Featured paper · Neuron 2026

Representational similarity modulates neural and behavioral signatures of novelty

Becker S, Modirshanechi A, Gerstner W

Novelty is not a fixed property of a stimulus. It depends on how similar the stimulus is to what a learner has already represented. This view reconciles conflicting findings across neural and behavioral studies.

Novelty depends on what you have already learned

A theory of novelty defined over a learner's internal representation. It explains how similarity to past experience shapes novelty signals in the brain and novelty-driven behavior.
